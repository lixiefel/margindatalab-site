Honest by design

Arithmetic, not a forecast.

1

A floor is not a price to print

A cost-plus floor ignores what your customers will pay and what other sellers charge.

2

Guests show their reaction later

How your guests react to a new price only shows after the change, and at small volumes that takes a long time to see.

3

App rates are labelled

Published rates are used where they exist. Where an app does not publish its rate, the screen says it is an estimate.
