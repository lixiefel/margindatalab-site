  • PB1/PBJT is added on the bill, so it is not taken out of the menu price.
  • QRIS fee for a UMI merchant on 80% of sales (0% for a micro business up to Rp 500.000 per transaction).
  • Target margin 65% of net; under 15% counts as thin.
  • Waste 3% of HPP; takeaway packaging Rp 1.500 on 30% of sales.
  • Cost-plus floors are rounded up to the next Rp 500 or Rp 1.000 so they never fall below the cost-plus figure; rounding does not make them prices, and they never cut a price. This is arithmetic, not a forecast: at a higher price some customers may buy less.
  • A cost-plus floor is the lowest price at which an item would reach the target margin: your costs divided by (1 - target margin). It is not a price to print: it ignores what customers will pay and what other sellers charge, so an item below its floor may need a change of cost, portion or place on the menu rather than a higher price.
